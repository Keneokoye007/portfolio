How I work

Six areas of expertise across business development, account growth and technology sales.

B2B prospecting and lead qualification

Researching target accounts, mapping decision-makers and identifying opportunities across subsidiaries. Using phone, email and LinkedIn outreach to start conversations and qualify prospects against their needs.

Consultative discovery and solution selling

Leading discovery conversations to understand business challenges, priorities and buying processes. Tailoring value propositions to each industry and stakeholder, and connecting customer needs with relevant solutions.
